# Continuity and language checks

## Before each scene

Check these against the plan, the character cards, the memory in the scene context and the previous scenes:

- **Time:** the day and hour from the plan, and how much time has passed since the previous scene.
- **Who is present:** only the characters in «Хто бере участь» ("who takes part"), plus anyone the plan clearly brings in. No one appears from nowhere.
- **Narrator:** if the scene context has a «ОПОВІДАЧ СЦЕНИ» ("scene narrator") section, it replaces the point of view from the style for this scene: first person through that character, third person through their perception only, or an omniscient narrator.
- **Objects:** what each character holds, carries, wears or left behind earlier.
- **State:** injuries, tiredness, sober or drunk, mood, powers that are recharging, and secrets they already know.
- **Facts already introduced:** names, places, rules of the world. Never contradict them, and never introduce something twice.
- **Appearance:** use only what the character card gives. Do not invent eye colour, height or scars that are not in the card. If you need a new detail, mark it as your decision.

## On the edit stage

In the scene's language, check:

- **Mixed alphabets:** Latin letters inside Cyrillic words (a, e, i, o, p, c, x look the same in both). Replace them.
- **Ukrainian prose:** calques and russianisms, for example «прийняти участь» → «взяти участь», «на протязі» → «протягом», «являється» → «є». Keep the author's deliberate style and characters' speech.
- **Invented appearance:** details of looks that are not in the character card. Remove them, or keep one and mark it as your decision.
- **Continuity:** objects and people that appear or disappear without a reason.

Report what you changed in one line. The edited text itself goes only through the edit stage.
