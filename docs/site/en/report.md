# Report a result

Here you can report a model result that breaks the [Terms of Use](legal.md) or the law, or a failure of the application's safeguards. In the Microsoft Store build, the "Report a result" link in the application's top bar leads here.

## How to report

Write to [report@obscurawriter.com](mailto:report@obscurawriter.com). It helps to include:

- the application version and where it is installed: Microsoft Store (the version is shown in Windows "Settings → Apps → Installed apps") or a working copy from GitHub;
- the provider and model of the stage, and the stage itself (plan, brief, draft, editing, memory, evaluation);
- what happened and what you expected: for example, the model produced forbidden content despite the story's age rating, or the character age safeguard did not trigger;
- a short excerpt of the result, only if the report cannot be understood without it.

Do not send provider keys or whole stories. The application sends nothing automatically: the project author sees only what you attach to your message.

## What happens next

- The project author reads every report. If the cause is in the application (a safeguard, a prompt, a model setting), it is fixed in one of the next updates.
- The text is produced by the model provider you chose. If the problem is in the model itself, the author may report the case to the provider without your personal data, and suggest another model to you.
- If a report concerns content that may be a crime, the project author may notify the competent authorities, as the Terms provide.

How the project author handles email is described on the [Privacy](privacy.md) page.

## Bugs without private content

Ordinary bugs and suggestions that contain no excerpts of your stories can also be filed publicly in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ysilvestrov/obscura-text-writer/issues).
